# Qualification rubric (placeholder)

Score each lead on the criteria below. Define your own weights and thresholds in `config/config.yaml`.

- Pain: is there a clear, repeated, costly problem?
- Fit: does the problem match an offer you deliver?
- Budget signals: does the post or profile show spend?
- Urgency: is there a deadline or trigger?
- Access: can you reach a decision maker directly?
- Red flags: scope creep, unpaid trials, unclear ownership.

Output: score, tier (pursue / watch / pass), and one-line reason.
